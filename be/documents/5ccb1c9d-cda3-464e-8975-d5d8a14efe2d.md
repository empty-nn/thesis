Thanh Hoa is a city in the [Central Coast](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Central_Coast_(Vietnam)) region of [Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vietnam). It is a major crossroads for buses to the [Lao](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Laos) border at Na Meo, but otherwise the town has little of interest to the average tourist. Thanh Hoa is sort of just a city, no more, no less. It's hospitable and reliable, but not particularly interesting.

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Thanh_Hoa&action=edit§ion=1)]

You can get here by bus or by train from Hanoi. The city is 153 km south of Hanoi, about 3 hours by bus or 3½ hours by train. Bus tickets are 100,000-120,000 [dong](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vietnam#Money) for a seat on air-con bus. The road is good. Train tickets from 100,000 dong for air-con hard-seat class.

You can also get in from Xam Nua (Sam Neua), Laos for 180,000 [kip](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Laos#Money) via the Na Meo border crossing. It takes about 10-12 hours along winding mountain roads in a small bus with padded seats. The aisle will be full of rice bags.

## Get around

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Thanh_Hoa&action=edit§ion=2)]

Best choice in every town in Vietnam to get around is "xe ôm" (moto-taxi). Negotiate price before you ride. For a trip of 5 km, pay 50,000 dong. Meter taxis are available with the same price throughout the country, call +84 37 858585 or dial 858585 if using a land line phone in town.

There are town buses you can use to get to the bus station and other areas around town.

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Thanh_Hoa&action=edit§ion=3)]

Sam Son Beach is located in Sam Son town, a small provincial town 16 km from Thanh Hoa. The beach is crowded from Apr-Sep. There are buses from Thanh Hoa to the town, departing every 30 minutes from the bus station.

Ben En National Park is a good destination for eco-tours. This park is 46 km southwest of Thanh Hoa. It is 16,000 ha of mountains and jungles with many kinds of wild animals and tropical trees. There is a big lake in the park, covering 4,000 ha containing 21 islands.
