Bao Loc is in the [Central Highlands (Vietnam)](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Central_Highlands_(Vietnam)).

## Understand

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Bao_Loc&action=edit§ion=1)]

This is a medium-sized city that sits on Hwy 20 between Ho Chi Minh and Dalat. This serves as a good resting stop if traveling on motorbike with many cafes and hotels with free Wi-Fi.

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Bao_Loc&action=edit§ion=4)]

The park in the middle of the city is quite nice. There's a lake (hồ Bao Loc) in the centre - a common place for locals to spend their weekend fishing. This makes a nice photo with the lake in the front and Vietnamese apartments (and a temple) in the back.

## Do

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Bao_Loc&action=edit§ion=5)]

There's not much to do other than eat, sleep, and enjoy the little park.
