Mong Cai is a coastal town on the [Chinese](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/China) border in [Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vietnam). Its Chinese neighbor is [Dongxing](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Dongxing).

You would probably consider Mong Cai only at most as a stopping point during your journey from China to [Ha Long Bay](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ha_Long_Bay) or from Vietnam to China. However, it is a pleasant town, and the nearby Trà Cổ Peninsula has one of northeastern Vietnam's few good beaches that can be accessed by road (without taking a ferry boat).

### From Dongxing, China

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Mong_Cai&action=edit§ion=2)]

If you wish to come to Viernam from Dongxing, the customary manner to do this is by walking. You may leave your car or motorbike on Chinese side for a few yuans. Then you cross the border by walking and take a motorbike taxi or similar to your destination.

See the instructions at [Ha Long Bay#Get in](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ha_Long_Bay#Get_in).

## Do

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Mong_Cai&action=edit§ion=6)]
- Visit the nearby Trà Cổ Peninsula, with its mostly uncrowded 15-km long beach, and a lovely Gothic church. The main tourist area on the peninsula, Trà Cổ town, with hotels and restaurants, is less than 10 km from Mong Cai; bus service is available.

## Buy

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Mong_Cai&action=edit§ion=7)]
