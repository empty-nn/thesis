Appearance

My Tho, is the capital of Tien Giang Province in [southern Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Southern_Vietnam). It is the first city in the [Mekong Delta](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Mekong_Delta) travellers from [Ho Chi Minh City](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh_City) arrive at and hence a popular spot for dropping tourists off for one of the many Mekong boat tours. For its proximity to [Ho Chi Minh City](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh_City) everything is more expensive here than at towns further inside the delta such as [Vinh Long](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vinh_Long) and [Can Tho](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Can_Tho).

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=My_Tho&action=edit§ion=1)]

There are lots of buses daily from Bến xe Miền Tây bus station in [Ho Chi Minh City](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh_City) to My Tho's bus station My Tho Ben Xe. Fares are around 60,000 dong for the express bus (2 hours) or 33,000 dong for the local bus (3 hours). From the station to the center of the town should cost 10,000 dong on a motorbike or even less with a local bus.

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=My_Tho&action=edit§ion=3)]

[1](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Special:Map/17/10.38315/106.6775/en)Royal Mausoleums (Lăng Hoàng Gia), Long Hưng, in Gò Công town. Sa Su 07:30-18:30. The mausoleum complex comprises the tomb of 19th-century duke Pham Dang Hung. The architecture is a blend of Western style, Chinese and traditional Hue.[2](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Special:Map/17/10.36287/106.37354/en)Vinh Trang Pagoda. Vinh Trang Pagoda is in a large garden in My Phong Commune. The architecture of the pagoda is a harmonious combination of Asian and European types of architecture, creating the splendid, but simple features of this Buddhist pagoda. Vinh Trang Pagoda is the greatest pagoda of Tien Giang Province.

## Do

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=My_Tho&action=edit§ion=4)]

River Islands boat tour: official rate is USD25 per person. Touts on the waterfront will offer tours for less. An old man on a bicycle has best deal, USD10 for two people.- Cross the Mekong to Ben Tre province to buy some banana candy. In this province, you can see family-run cacao plantations (off the beaten track).
Cai Be floating market: this is a main market in the Mekong Delta. Cai Be is 35km west of My Tho[on the Tien River](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=10.3332&mlon=106.0359#map=10/10.3332/106.0359). Open all day 5-18h. Tourists can find any kind of fruits at this market, and all of them are cheap. Along the river there are many gardens. At night, there will be junks with lighted lanterns which make the river vivid.- Take a trip along
Tien River: tourists can visit villages along the river. Dong Tam Snake-Breeding Farm: Dong Tam is the centre of snake breeding for taking venom for export, as well as medicinal plant cultivation and the study and treatment of snake bites. There are also crocodiles and other species of birds and animals on hand.
