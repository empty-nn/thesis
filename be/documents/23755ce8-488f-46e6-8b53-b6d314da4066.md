Chau Doc (Châu Đốc) is a city in the heart of the Mekong Delta, in [Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vietnam).

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=1)]

Chau Doc is best-known to travelers as a border crossing with [Cambodia](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Cambodia).

### By bus

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=2)]

There are various daily bus connections to Chau Doc by different bus companies, including the ubiquitous and good quality Futa / Phuong Trang buses, leaving from popular destinations such as Ho Chi Minh City, Can Tho and Ha Tien.

To and from Phnom Penh, Cambodia, there are some buses, but it looks like they can't be booked online. Ask at your accommodation, or take the speedboat.

When arriving at the bus terminal, no grab cars and no car taxis seem to be available to get to the city. You can either walk (which might take only around 20 mins if close to the bus station) or bargain with the motorbike taxi drivers which will readily make ridiculously expensive offers for taking you and your luggage to town. If you offer 50% of their initial price they will immediately accept, so try starting lower than that. A Grab bike for the same route may cost only around 20k dong (Jan 2025).

### By speedboat

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=3)]

There is only one [ferry company](https://hangchautourist.vn/) as of 2025 which does daily transfers from and to Phnom Penh. The journey takes 4 hours plus 2 hours at the border crossing. eVisa are not accepted when entering into Cambodia, but paper visa should work and the crew will get visa on arrival for everyone else. Boat ride costs 40 US$, VOA costs 36 US$ (Jan 2025).

## Get around

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=4)]

Walk: the main downtown area is an easy walk, from the riverside to the market to Chau Doc Square.

### By motorcycle

The owner of the guesthouse speaks quite a good English.

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=6)]

A stroll along the riverside will be well worth it. You will bump into many young people eager (but a bit shy) to engage you in conversation. It can be very rewarding after a morning spent haggling in the colourful markets.

## Do

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=7)]
- A traditional massage with suction cups is a great way to relax and unwind before returning to Go Chi Minh City or taking one of the riverboats to Phnom Penh.

[1](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Special:Map/17/10.675242/105.076348/en) Sam Mountain (6 km from Chau Doc). Bike to Sam Mountain: rent a bike from your hotel (USD1-2) and ride 6 m to the base of Sam Mountain. It's an easy ride over flat paved and gravel road, just watch out for traffic. Walk on food from the base of the mountain, up the stairs to the top. Many viewpoints and cafe's with hammocks along the way to stop for a drink and a rest. Alternatively you can take a moto straight to the top of Sam Mountain, a great place to catch the sunset and see the views of Vietnam and into Cambodia.

[2](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Special:Map/17/10.588113/105.056681/en) Tra Su Bird Sanctuary (about 25 km from Chau Doc). As of January 2015 most hotels and travel agents are charging around USD34 for a two person trip to Tra Su (Includes moto drivers and boat fees). If you speak to any of the moto drivers around the covered market you should be able to hire one for around 150,000 - 200,000 dong per moto and can visit Sam Mountain or the Pagoda on the way back. Some of the drivers speak good English. Hiring a driver on your own will save about USD8-10 for a two person trip (depending on your negotiating skills). 75,000 dong per person for 1½ hour visit (includes both speed and row boat ride).

## Buy

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=8)]

A large, sprawling market dominates the centre of town, with a covered section in Bach Dang street. There are many ATMs, mainly in center near the pagoda, and on Le Loi/riverside.

## Eat

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=9)]

Food in Chau Doc is dominated by catches from the Mekong.
- Thanh Tinh, 13 Quang Trung (close to the indoor market). 06:00-21:00. A nice and clean local restaurant that serves vegan food. Friendly and English-speaking staff. They can also prepare take-away meals (handy for the journey on the slow boat to Cambodia). They charged 95,000 dong for a 'com phan', almost three times the usual price (March 2011). 25,000-40,000 dong.
- Bay Bong, 22 Duong Thuong Dang Le (with the indoor market on your right hand side, continue until you can only go left or right; go right there and you'll eventually see it on your left), ☏
[+84 76 867 271](tel:+8476867271). Good food with a very long menu; locals and visitors eat here. The atmosphere isn't particularly romantic. 25,000-55,000 dong.

## Drink

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=10)]

Chau Giang Floating Restaurant, Trần Hưng Đạo street. You can get a cheap drink with a great view of the river at the Chau Giang Floating Restaurant, on the pier at the opposite end to the Victoria Hotel, near the Hang Chau ferry office.

## Connect

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=14)]

Get a prepaid SIM card from Mobifone (centre, near pagoda) or Vinaphone (Le Loi, riverside): about 65,000 dong and monthly Internet package ~120,000 dong.

Have a problem with your phone, buy a new SIM card or recharge account balance, visit The Anh Mobile Service 31 Le Loi (near Chau Doc Church, church three-way crossroads).

## Go next

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Chau_Doc&action=edit§ion=15)]

Bus station is near the entrance of city about 4 km from centre and ~20,000 dong moto taxi ride.

[Can Tho](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Can_Tho)is a popular next stop for travelers on a tour of the Mekong Delta. 125,000 dong, 3 hr[Ha Tien](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ha_Tien)can be an intermediate stop on the way to[Phu Quoc](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Phu_Quoc), although it's hard to find a connection online. Try in your accommodation or at a travel agent.[Ho Chi Minh City](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh_City). Phuong Trang bus company (phone +84 76 356 5888) make the connection for 125,000 dong 6 times a day between 08:45 to 00:15.[Phnom Penh](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Phnom_Penh)is another popular destination. Many travelers stay for one night in Chau Doc and go the next day to Cambodia. See the Get In section for how to get there. Try not to take too many dong with you, because the exchange rate you get at the border is not very fair. There is no ATM, so bring enough dollars with you. At the border, there is a small restaurant, but it is comparably expensive. You can get take-away meals and a bottle of water from restaurants in Chau Doc if you plan ahead.
