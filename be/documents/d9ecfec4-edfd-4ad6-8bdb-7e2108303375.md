Pu Luong Nature Reserve is a mountainous rural area in Thanh Hoa Province, [Northern Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Northern_Vietnam). It is known for its rice terraces, traditional Thai ethnic villages, bamboo water wheels, and quiet trekking routes through forests and valleys.

## Understand

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Pu_Luong&action=edit§ion=1)]

Pu Luong offers a more peaceful alternative to Sapa, with fewer tourists and a stronger sense of local life. The area is home to ethnic Thai and Muong communities who maintain traditional farming and handicraft practices.

The landscape features limestone mountains, jungle, rice terraces, and small villages scattered across valleys.

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Pu_Luong&action=edit§ion=2)]

Pu Luong is about 4–5 hours from Hanoi.

Shared limousine vans depart daily from Hanoi Old Quarter. Private cars are also available. Routes often pass through Mai Chau.

## Get around

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Pu_Luong&action=edit§ion=3)]

The best way to explore Pu Luong is on foot.

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Pu_Luong&action=edit§ion=4)]
- Rice terraces – best viewed in May–June and September–October when the fields turn green or golden
- Bamboo water wheels – traditional irrigation systems commonly found along local streams
- Hieu Waterfall, Ban Hieu village (motorbike access is possible from the main road toward Ban Hieu village). The waterfall is known for its natural pools featuring multiple cascading levels and natural swimming spots, jungle scenery, and peaceful atmosphere. It is one of the best-known natural attractions in Pu Luong Nature Reserve, with water flowing through several levels of limestone rocks surrounded by forest and traditional stilt houses.
- Kho Muong village (it can be reached from other villages in Pu Luong by local roads and trekking routes). A remote valley village surrounded by limestone mountains, home to Bat Cave, a limestone cave near the village. The village is surrounded by limestone mountains and rice terraces, and is known for its peaceful atmosphere and traditional Thai ethnic culture.

## Do

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Pu_Luong&action=edit§ion=5)]
- Trekking between villages such as Don, Uoi, Kho Muong and Hieu
- Experience traditional stilt-house homestays
- Join local cooking activities
- Explore jungle trails and rice terrace landscapes
- Bamboo rafting (seasonal depending on water levels)

## Eat

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Pu_Luong&action=edit§ion=6)]

Local food is simple and based on fresh ingredients:
- Bamboo-cooked rice
- Grilled pork
- Mountain vegetables

Vegetarian options are often available in homestays.

## Sleep

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Pu_Luong&action=edit§ion=7)]

Accommodation options include:
- Traditional stilt-house homestays - Eco lodges with private rooms

## Stay safe

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Pu_Luong&action=edit§ion=8)]

Paths can be slippery in rainy season. Good walking shoes are recommended.
