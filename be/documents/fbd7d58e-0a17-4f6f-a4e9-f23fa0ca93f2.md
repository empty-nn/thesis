## Tam Dao

(Tam Đảo) is a hill station 930 m above sea level in [Northern Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Northern_Vietnam). It's 70 km from Hanoi.

## Understand

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Tam_Dao&action=edit§ion=1)]

Tam Dao was founded by French colonialists in 1907 as a summer retreat from the heat of Hanoi.

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Tam_Dao&action=edit§ion=2)]

The cheapest way to reach Tam Dao from Hanoi is to take a local bus (30,000 dong, 1 hour) and a taxi to travel the last 20 km (US$10) or by motorbike taxi (80,000 dong). By private taxi from Hanoi, it's 700,000 dong (2 hours). (Prices as of 2012.)
