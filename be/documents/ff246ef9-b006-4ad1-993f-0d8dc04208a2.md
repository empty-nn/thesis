Phan Rang - Tháp Chàm is the capital of the Ninh Thuan province in the [Southern Region](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Southern_Vietnam) of [Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vietnam).

## Understand

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Phan_Rang%E2%80%93Th%C3%A1p_Ch%C3%A0m&action=edit§ion=1)]

Phan Rang - Tháp Chàm, then known as Panduranga, is a former capital of the Cham Empire. It lies along the 1A National Highway between [Nha Trang](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Nha_Trang) and [Phan Thiet](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Phan_Thiet) with many Cham people still living in the area. It has a domestic resort town nearby in the form of [Ninh Chu](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ninh_Chu?action=edit&redlink=1) beach, 8 km to the east. There are also restored and still in use Cham relics. It is a quieter alternative to Nha Trang to the north, [Dalat](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Dalat) to the west and [Phan Thiet](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Phan_Thiet) to the south with few foreign travelers stopping. Sometimes tour buses on the way to Dalat will make a lunch and Cham tower stop here.

A cog railway that ran to Dalat has been damaged since the war. A 7-km (4.3-mi) section of the line between Da Lat Railway Station and the nearby village of Trại Mát was restored and returned to active use as a tourist attraction. Restored railway cars carry the name "Dalat Plateau Rail Road", although this name was not used when the entire line was in use. A plan was announced in 2019 to restore the remainder of the line.

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Phan_Rang%E2%80%93Th%C3%A1p_Ch%C3%A0m&action=edit§ion=4)]

Po Kluang Garai Cham Tower 8 km west of the town on the road to [Dalat](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Dalat) near the Thap Cham train station. This is the last capital of the Cham empire before the Vietnamese took over. The location has been restored and has an interesting photography gallery in their otherwise tchotchke filled souvenir area.

Po Rame is a harder to find tower 9 km south on Highway 1A and off the highway a bit in a village. The temple is still in use although the people tending to it will usually open it up to let you in.

## Eat

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Phan_Rang%E2%80%93Th%C3%A1p_Ch%C3%A0m&action=edit§ion=7)]

Phan Rang–Tháp Chàm is a seafood-heavy city, where various kinds of fish, prawns and squid are all staples.

The stretch of [Ninh Chu](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ninh_Chu?action=edit&redlink=1) beach next to the Sai Gon - Ninh Chu Hotel has a bunch of local restaurants with live seafood in bowls and tanks for reasonable prices (50,000 - 150,000 dong). Various roadside stalls serve up pho, bun, and com tam (broken rice with grilled pork chop) for 15,000 - 25,000 dong. The hordes of goats you see wandering roads are served in the restaurants. These prices are accurate as of June 2012.

[1](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Special:Map/17/11.613/109.147/en)[Sorrento Beach Club Vietnam and Kite Centre](http://sorrentobeachclub.com/), Ninh Hai Phan Rang, Thap Cham, My Hoa, ☏[+84 333 317 122](tel:+84333317122). Serves both Vietnamese and international cuisine.[2](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Special:Map/17/11.5706/108.9939/en)[Chu Ngo Chicken Rice](http://www.comgachungo.com/)(Cơm Gà Chú Ngộ), 62 - 64 Chế Lan Viên, Khu K1, ☏[+84 944 899 068](tel:+84944899068). Serves chicken rice, pork rice, chicken pho and other dishes.
