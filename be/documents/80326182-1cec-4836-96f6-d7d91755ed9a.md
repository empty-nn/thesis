Tra Vinh is a city in [southern Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Southern_Vietnam).

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Tra_Vinh&action=edit§ion=1)]

There are direct minibuses from Ho Chi Minh city. Another option is to arrive by boat. Either a private charter or you can try to get on a cargo boat from [Ben Tre](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ben_Tre).

## Get around

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Tra_Vinh&action=edit§ion=2)]

The centre itself is small enough to cover on foot. To explore the greater area you can rent a bike from the tourist office or via some hotels.

The tourist office sells a map (20,000 dong, Jan 2012) which covers both the city and Tra Vinh Province.

There is a place to rent motorbikes on Nguyen Thi Minh Khai 42B (about 5-10 minute walk from the centre). The shop is called Nguyet. Price for a decent automatic is 200,000 dong, a manual transmission is 100,000-150,000 dong. (Jan 2012)

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Tra_Vinh&action=edit§ion=3)]

There is a big market right in the centre. The city also has some Khmer pagodas.

## Do

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Tra_Vinh&action=edit§ion=4)]

Explore the centre on foot, or rent a bike to see the province of Tra Vinh. The beach is supposed to be one of the nicest in the Mekong Delta.
