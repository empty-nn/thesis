Vĩnh Long is the capital of Vinh Long Province in [southern Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Southern_Vietnam). It is located on one of the many branches of the Mekong River.

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=1)]

### By road

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=2)]

Vinh Long is 70 km from Mytho, 34 km from Can Tho and 136 km from Ho Chi Minh City. National Hwy 1A and 53 run through the province.

### By bus

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=3)]

There are buses between Vinh Long and Ho Chi Minh City (3 hr 30 min, [200,000 dong](https://xetuanhiep.com/dat-ve-truc-tuyen?from=61&to=29&departDate=&fromLabel=Vinh%20Long&toLabel=Ho%20Chi%20Minh)(2024)), Can Tho (1 hr, 50,000 dong (2017)), Mytho, Long Xuyen, Sa Dec, Rach Gia, and other Mekong Delta destinations.

### By boat

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=4)]

There are no scheduled boats anymore. However, it is possible to book a tourist boat to [Chau Doc](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Chau_Doc) including a night on the Mekong (US$20) and a bus transfer to [Phnom Penh](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Phnom_Penh) (additional US$6).

## Get around

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=5)]
- The ferry to An Binh costs 1000 dong if you are on foot (July 2025), and up to 2,500 dong if with a motorbike. Departs every 15-20 min.

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=6)]
- Small Chinese temple, Cua Thienh Hau, on the east bank, not far from the southeastern bridges.

## Do

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=7)]
- Mekong Boat Tour. The thing to do in Vinh Long. A typical trip includes the floating market at Cai Be and An Binh Islands lasting about 4 hr. US$15-25.
- Visit An Binh. If you are on budget or you already have done enough boat tours, you can go to An Binh on the ferry (1000 dong, one-way) and either walk on the main islands (small bridges connect nearly all the islands), rent bicycle/moto or pay a mototaxi (40-60,000 dong per hour). Bonsai garden is about 20 min from the ferry pier by motorbike. Near the pier there is a Chinese temple and a school, a bit further on, a church.

## Eat

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=9)]

You can easily buy food from street stalls or in the market in town centre.

There is a converted boat restaurant moored opposite the plaza in front of the central supermarket. It goes on a small jaunt on the Mekong, prices from US$3-20 per head depending on what you choose à la carte.

## Sleep

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Vinh_Long&action=edit§ion=11)]

The accommodations are a bit scattered throughout the town. Some hotels can be found along the main traffic arteries leading to the town centre. There are a couple of options in the city centre. Mosquitoes can be a nuisance in this area, however, hotels rarely provide mosquito nets, so bring your own.
- Lạc Long, 2G Hùng Vương, P.1 (5 min walk from inner bus station), ☏
[+84 70 383 6846 - 3822099](tel:+84703836846-3822099). Decent no frills rooms. Apart from the standard features such as TV, fridge, phone, private bath with hot water, it also has Wi-Fi. All the rooms are available fan or air-con. From 120,000 dong. - Phựng Hoàng, 2H Hùng Vương, ☏
[+84 703 825 185](tel:+84703825185). Check-out: 12:00. Choice of comforts as the prices indicate. This place is popular with backpackers. Cheapest room is large and includes TV, fridge, private bath with hot walter, Wi-Fi, and air-con. From 160,000 dong (Mar 2015).

Several places on the island offer what they call homestays. These may not be in actual local people's homes, but still in traditional-style houses right in the countryside. One of them is

## Green Island Tour

(+84 70 385 9908), offering an overnight stay including a large dinner consisting of many different dishes, breakfast and a bicycle for US$10. It's excellent value considering the dinner alone would cost almost the same in any restaurant. A moto from the bus station can take you here for 50,000 dong including the ferry ride.
