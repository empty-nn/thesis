## Cẩm Phả

is a port city in the Red River Delta region of [Northern Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Northern_Vietnam). Cẩm Phả's non-industrial area is known as one of the 10 most famous landscapes in Vietnam

## Understand

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Cam_Pha&action=edit§ion=1)]

Its name Cẩm-phả (锦普) means "splendor and spaciousness" in Hanese text.

In 2019, it was home to about 190,000 people.

Cẩm Phả's strategic location on the northeastern coast made it a target of bombing multiple times by US aircraft during the [Vietnam War](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vietnam_War).
