## Long Xuyen

(Long Xuyên) is the capital city of An Giang Province, in the [Mekong Delta](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Southern_Vietnam) region of southwestern [Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vietnam).

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Long_Xuyen&action=edit§ion=1)]

### By bus

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Long_Xuyen&action=edit§ion=2)]

Long Xuyen can be best reached by bus, which takes about 5 hours from [Ho Chi Minh City](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh_City) or 1½ hours from [Can Tho](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Can_Tho).

## Get around

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Long_Xuyen&action=edit§ion=3)]

The city itself is not as large as its close neighbor [Can Tho](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Can_Tho) and the centre of town is easily walkable. Like any other Vietnamese city there are plenty of xe-om motorcycle drivers or a bicycle with cart attached (xe loi), which is a specialty of the Mekong.

Another easy mode of transportation is renting a bike from a streetside bike repairman, although most foreigners would prefer a motorbike to go farther into the province when time permits.
