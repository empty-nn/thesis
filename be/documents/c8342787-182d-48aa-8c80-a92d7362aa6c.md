Appearance

## Kham Duc

is in the [Central Highlands](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Central_Highlands_(Vietnam)) of [Vietnam](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Vietnam).

## Understand

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Kh%C3%A2m_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c&action=edit§ion=1)]

A small sleepy town, 2 hours ride from Hoi An toward the central highlands and Pleiku. There is a war memorial in this town, along the Ho Chi Minh Road.

## Get in

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Kh%C3%A2m_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c&action=edit§ion=2)]

The town is on the Ho Chi Minh Road, so buses coming from either way can drop you here and is easy to flag one down to leave.

## See

[[edit](https://en.wikivoyage.org/w/index.php?title=Kh%C3%A2m_%C4%90%E1%BB%A9c&action=edit§ion=4)]

Not much to see in this town even though the mountainous road from Hoi An toward Pleiku and Ia Drang valley crossing this town. Worth a visit. The town is on the crossroad of several motorcycle trails.
